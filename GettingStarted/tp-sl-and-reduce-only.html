    TP/SL and Reduce-only¶

    Take-profit (TP) and stop-loss (SL) groups create conditional exits from a symbol's net position in one trading subaccount. Each group has a quantity; it does not automatically resize to cover the entire position as other orders trade. Exits are reduce-only: they cannot increase exposure or reverse the position at settlement.

    Choose an activation mode¶

    Mode When protection starts Exit quantity
    parent_fill (0 on the signed wire) After a bound parent fill settles successfully Settled parent fills, capped at signed group qty, less successfully settled exits
    immediate (1 on the signed wire) On approval, without a parent order Submitted qty, limited by the remaining position at settlement

    For immediate, the subaccount must already have a position that the exit side reduces. The web trading application's Protect position action defaults to the full position size displayed at submission and accepts a smaller TP/SL quantity. Later unrelated fills do not resize that group. The web order form also accepts a TP/SL quantity in base units for a new parent order; leaving it blank uses the full parent quantity. TP alone, SL alone, or both can be submitted. A position may have multiple independently sized groups, each cancelled separately. Reduce-only settlement prevents their combined exits from reversing the position.

    For parent_fill, the parent must match the group's wallet, subaccount, and symbol, have at least the group's quantity, and have the opposite side. Protection grows only from that parent's successfully settled fills, capped at the signed group quantity. Parent fills beyond that cap are unprotected by this group. Existing exposure and fills from other orders are not added to the group's quantity. A provisional or busted parent fill does not add protection.

    Example: existing exposure plus a parent order¶

    You are long 2 units and submit a buy parent for 1 with TP/SL. After it fills and settles, you are long 3, but that group has an exit quantity of 1. If its exit fully settles, you remain long 2. The exit reduces the net position; there is no separately identified lot being closed.

    To protect all 3 units, cancel the existing dormant group, wait for its cancellation, and submit an immediate group with qty corresponding to 3. Read the current account state before choosing that quantity.

    Submit TP/SL¶

    Authenticate, subscribe to the account stream, and use current symbology to encode prices and quantities. The conditional-group reference defines the 196-byte signed proof, including its activation_mode byte. Enable TP, SL, or both. Disabled leg fields must be zero.

    For an existing position:

    1. Choose the opposite exit side: SELL for a long, BUY for a short.
    2. Sign a group with activation_mode = 1, the exit quantity, trigger prices, trigger sources, and expiry.
    3. Send signed_conditional_group with JSON envelope version: 1.
    4. Wait for ConditionalApproved. Do not submit a parent.

    For activation by a new parent order:

    1. Sign and submit the group with activation_mode = 0.
    2. Wait for ConditionalApproved and retain its group_external_order_id.
    3. Send signed_conditional_parent using that group ID and a normal order proof with quantity at least the group's.
    4. Observe the parent's OrderAccepted or OrderReject, then settlement. Triggers become eligible only after a successful parent settlement.

    A rejected binding does not cancel the group. For example, a parent quantity of 1 cannot bind to a group signed for 2; the group stays in waiting_parent. Submit an eligible parent or cancel that unused group. Rejection after successful binding instead completes an unprotected group with ConditionalCompleted.

    A disconnect or timeout does not establish whether a submission succeeded. Reconnect and reconcile the account snapshot and lifecycle events.

    Trigger prices and exit orders¶

    Trigger sources are a bit mask: 1 = oracle, 2 = completed-auction fill price, 3 = either. A selected source triggers when its observed price reaches or passes the threshold:

    Position Exit side TP triggers SL triggers
    Long SELL Price >= TP price Price <= SL price
    Short BUY Price <= TP price Price >= SL price

    When both legs are enabled, SL must be below TP for a long position and above TP for a short position. Prices must satisfy the market price grid. The latest cached price is also evaluated when settled parent exposure becomes protected or residual exposure rearms.

    The selected leg becomes a TAKER, REDUCE_ONLY order:

    • TP becomes a GTC limit order whose limit is the TP trigger price.
    • SL becomes an unrestricted market IOC order (price = 0, delta_ppm = 0). The trigger price is not a guaranteed execution price or a slippage bound.

    A TP child carries the group's signed expiry. An SL child has a matching deadline of the earlier of the group expiry and five seconds after activation; its unfilled remainder is cancelled after its eligible auction.

    When a leg triggers, its sibling is removed from trigger storage and emits a CancelAck. ConditionalTriggered.selected_external_order_id supplies the new active child ID, replacing the selected dormant leg ID. Use that ID for the child's ordinary OrderAccepted, fills, settlements, and cancellation. This applies on the first trigger and every later trigger. A trigger does not guarantee order admission or execution.

    After reconnecting, a snapshot group's pending_leg, when set to 1 = TP or 2 = SL, identifies the selected leg; that leg's external order ID is the active child ID. Reconcile working orders and lifecycle events to determine whether it is still live. Order history can associate children with their group through conditional_group_external_order_id and conditional_leg when available.

    Rearming remaining protection¶

    Rearming restores conditional triggers after the active child has no remaining order quantity and all its matched fills have resolved. Both activation modes can rearm if protected quantity remains and the group has not been cancelled, invalidated, or expired. Failed child settlement does not consume protection. In parent_fill mode, parent fills settling while the child is active can also leave quantity for another cycle.

    For example, a buy parent for 2 units settles 1 and its TP child activates for 1. The second parent unit settles while that child is active. Once the child fully matches and successfully settles its 1 unit, the group can rearm for the remaining 1.

    A partially matched TP remains a live GTC limit order; it does not create a second child while its remainder is live. If an SL matches only part of its quantity, cancellation of the unfilled IOC remainder terminates the group with ConditionalCancelled, after pending fills resolve. It does not rearm that cancelled remainder.

    Rearming emits ConditionalApproved. A parent-fill group with no protected quantity can instead wait for further parent fills in waiting_fill state. The next ConditionalTriggered supplies the new active child ID. Approval and snapshot qty fields retain the original signed group quantity; they do not report current remaining protection. The active child's OrderAccepted reports the quantity submitted for that trigger.

    Manual trades and closing the position¶

    Unrelated normal trades do not resize or cancel a TP/SL group. Continuing the long-3 example above, a manual sell of 1 leaves you long 2 with the same 1-unit group. A later fully settled TP/SL exit leaves you long 1.

    Alternatively, closing the full long-3 position behaves differently according to the manual order's mode:

    Manual close TP/SL behavior after successful settlement makes the position flat
    Reduce-only sell 3 Groups protecting that subaccount, symbol, and position direction are invalidated; active children are cancelled as part of resolution
    Normal sell 3 The unrelated trade does not automatically cancel the groups; they can remain armed

    The web trading application's Close position action uses a reduce-only market IOC order. A partial reduce-only close that leaves exposure does not automatically resize unrelated groups.

    While flat, a remaining TP/SL exit cannot settle a new short position. If you open another long before the old group triggers or expires, that exit can reduce the new position. Explicitly cancel unwanted groups after a normal manual close, and confirm their lifecycle events before reusing the position.

    Change or cancel protection¶

    Groups are immutable. Cancel and replace a group to change its quantity, trigger prices, sources, or expiry.

    • Before activation, send signed_conditional_cancel with the group ID and wait for ConditionalCancelled.
    • After activation, cancel the live child with signed_cancel using the ID from ConditionalTriggered.selected_external_order_id. A successful user child cancellation terminates the bracket and prevents residual exposure from rearming. A group cancel can be rejected while activation is in progress.
    • Cancelling a parent stops new fills. Successfully settled fills retain protection; pending fills are resolved through settlement. If no protected quantity or pending fills remain, the group completes.
    • A successful parent modification invalidates its group. An already active child remains an ordinary live order that the trader must monitor or cancel.

    Group expiry is at most 90 days after gateway admission. Dormant expiry emits ConditionalExpired; an active child's rejection or expiry terminates its group. Use account lifecycle events and reconnect snapshots to reconcile state, rather than inferring group state from individual leg cancellations.

    Submit and reconcile reduce-only orders¶

    Set execution_mode = 4 inside the signed ContractOrderV1 proof, then send the usual signed_order envelope. There is no top-level reduce_only request field. A modify must preserve the live order's execution mode; cancel and replace to change between normal and reduce-only.

    Reduce-only sells can settle only against a long position; buys can settle only against a short. Settlement clips a provisional match to the available reducing capacity of each reduce-only side, including zero while flat or when the position has the wrong direction. It never expands the submitted quantity.

    For example, if a provisional sell matches 2 units but the long position is only 1 at settlement, FillSettled reports qty = 2, settled_qty = 1, and reduced_qty = 1. The resulting position is flat. Fees are scaled to the settled quantity and reported in applied_fee.

    For these settlement events, reconcile positions and fees using settled_qty and applied_fee. qty and fee retain the provisional match and requested fee. qty = settled_qty + reduced_qty; reduced quantity is distinct from a failed-settlement FillBusted. The reduce_only_leg, limiting_leg, and exhausted_leg booleans describe this account's side of the settlement.

    Reference-client support¶

    The C++ reference TUI supports immediate and parent-fill TP/SL, cancellation of existing groups or triggered children, and reduce-only order submission. See its usage guide. The Python reference TUI also supports these actions; see its usage guide. See the API reference for the signed command formats.
